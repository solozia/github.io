About me

I am a Senior Lecturer in Computer Science at the Department of Computing & Games, School of Computing, Engineering & Digital Technologies (SCEDT), Teesside University. Before transitioning to academia, I worked as a postdoctoral researcher at Dublin City University and University College Cork, Ireland, and as a part-time teacher at the National University of Ireland Galway.

My research interests encompass Cybersecurity, AI Governance, Secure and Safe AI, and the Internet of Things (IoT). I take an interdisciplinary approach that bridges academia and industry through innovative research with practical societal impact.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Galway (formerly National University of Ireland Galway), funded by Science Foundation Ireland. I hold a Master of Engineering degree from Hankuk University of Foreign Studies, South Korea (2011-2013), where I was awarded a Foreign Student Scholarship. Before academia, I worked for over 7 years in the IT industry in Bangladesh, serving as a Technical Consultant, Project Manager, and Senior Network & Security Engineer, leading major government projects including Bangladesh's Machine Readable Passport & Visa (MRP & V) and the Bangladesh Automated Clearing House (BACH) for the Central Bank.

As a recognized academic leader, I frequently participate in professional discussions, including invited guest appearances on industry-led panels, podcasts, and discussions focusing on cybersecurity, AI governance, and AI ethics. I am a Senior Member of IEEE, a member of the Elsevier Advisory Panel, and actively contribute to the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) and various IEEE communities including Artificial Intelligence, IoT, Cloud Computing, and Big Data.

Research Interests

My interdisciplinary research lies at the intersection of Cybersecurity, Artificial Intelligence (AI) Ethics & Safety, and IoT. My main research interests include:

  • AI-Driven Cybersecurity - Developing intelligent systems for threat detection, model hardening, adversarial defense, and secure AI implementations
  • AI Ethics and Safety - Exploring frameworks for explainability, trustworthy AI, and systemic safety, particularly in high-stakes domains like healthcare
  • AI Governance - Investigating exploratory and evolutionary game-theoretical approaches to AI governance, safety, and multi-agent systems
  • Internet of Things (IoT) - Enhancing the resilience of IoT networks against emerging threats and vulnerabilities, including Social Internet of Things (SIoT)
  • Adversarial Machine Learning - Examining multi-agent collusion risks and adversarial consensus in AI-based systems
  • Semantic Web & Linked Data - Web of Objects (WoO), ontology-based systems, and real-time stream data analytics

I am particularly interested in bridging academic research with practical implementations to address real-world challenges affecting SMEs, local communities, and healthcare systems. My work combines theoretical foundations with applied solutions that create meaningful societal impact.
