I am a Senior Lecturer in Computer Science at the Department of Computing & Games, School of Computing, Engineering & Digital Technologies (SCEDT), Teesside University. My research encompasses Cybersecurity, AI Governance, Secure and Safe AI, and the Internet of Things (IoT). I take an interdisciplinary approach that bridges academia and industry through innovative research with practical societal impact.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Galway (formerly National University of Ireland Galway), funded by Science Foundation Ireland, and my Master of Engineering degree from Hankuk University of Foreign Studies, South Korea. Before academia, I worked for over 7 years in the IT industry in Bangladesh, leading major government projects including Bangladesh's Machine Readable Passport & Visa and the Bangladesh Automated Clearing House for the Central Bank.

As a recognized academic leader, I frequently participate in professional discussions, including invited guest appearances on industry-led panels, podcasts, and discussions focusing on cybersecurity, AI governance, and AI ethics. I am a Senior Member of IEEE, a member of the British Computer Society, Elsevier Advisory Panel, and the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).

Research Interests

My interdisciplinary research lies at the intersection of Cybersecurity, Artificial Intelligence (AI) Ethics & Safety, and IoT. My main research interests include:

  • AI-Driven Cybersecurity - Developing intelligent systems for threat detection, model hardening, adversarial defense, and secure AI implementations
  • AI Ethics and Safety - Exploring frameworks for explainability, trustworthy AI, and systemic safety in high-stakes domains like healthcare
  • Self-Healing AI Systems - Autonomous systems capable of detecting, diagnosing, and recovering from failures without human intervention
  • AI Governance - Investigating exploratory and evolutionary game-theoretical approaches to AI governance, safety, and multi-agent systems
  • Internet of Things (IoT) - Enhancing the resilience of IoT networks against emerging threats, including Social Internet of Things (SIoT)
  • Adversarial Machine Learning - Examining multi-agent collusion risks and adversarial consensus in AI-based systems
  • Semantic Web & Linked Data - Web of Objects (WoO), ontology-based systems, and real-time stream data analytics
